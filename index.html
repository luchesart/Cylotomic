How the check works

Starting with xⁿ − 1, divide by the cyclotomic factors for proper divisors and differentiate in integer arithmetic. Strip any root at zero. On each signed interval, compute Descartes’ bound after x = ±t/(1+t), t > 0, then verify opposite signs at both endpoints of every listed bracket. Equality of the upper and lower counts proves completeness and simplicity away from zero.
